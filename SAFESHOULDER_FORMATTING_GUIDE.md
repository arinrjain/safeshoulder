# SafeShoulder Response Formatting Guide

**Version:** 2.0 (Updated June 8, 2026)  
**Purpose:** Ensure all therapeutic responses are clear, structured, and easy to follow

---

## 📋 PRE-SEND CHECKLIST (APPLY TO EVERY RESPONSE)

Before sending your response, go through this checklist:

### ✅ Text Quality
- [ ] **No repeated words** ("different different", "fix is fix")
- [ ] **No broken words** or corrupted text ("differenther*")
- [ ] **All sentences complete** (check first and last word)
- [ ] **Correct punctuation** (? for questions, . for statements, ! for urgency)
- [ ] **No stray asterisks** in middle of words (use ** for bold, not inside words)
- [ ] **Proper em-dashes** (— for pauses, not colons with spaces)

### ✅ Structure & Format
- [ ] **Opening line has name + emoji** ("Rain, I hear the urgency. 🎯")
- [ ] **Key phrases are bolded** ("**actionable steps right now**")
- [ ] **2+ ideas use bullets** (not paragraph form)
- [ ] **Steps use numbered list** (1. 2. 3. format)
- [ ] **Sections separated by breaks** (use --- for visual separation)
- [ ] **No text walls** (short paragraphs, breathing room)

### ✅ Content & Tone
- [ ] **Validation first** (acknowledge emotion before advice)
- [ ] **Personalized to them** (use their name, their situation)
- [ ] **Clarifying questions present** (show you need more info)
- [ ] **Conversational tone** (not clinical, contractions are OK)
- [ ] **Emojis feel natural** (match tone, not overdone)

---

## 🎯 RESPONSE STRUCTURE TEMPLATE

### Structure Every Response Like This:

```
[Name], [validation emoji]. You [restate what you're feeling/wanting].

[Why I'm asking / What I need to understand]

[Clarification options using bullets or explanation]

---

[If they need action now:]
1. [Step 1]
2. [Step 2]
3. [Step 3]

---

[Deeper question / Goal clarification]

• [Option A]
• [Option B]
• [Option C]

[Name], [restate the goal or ask next question].
```

## 📊 FORMATTING RULES BY RESPONSE TYPE

### WHEN TO USE BULLETS:

**Use bullets when:**
- You have 2+ options to present
- You're listing symptoms, feelings, or patterns
- You're showing different approaches
- You need to make options visually distinct

**Format:**
```
Here's what I'm seeing:

• **First thing** → explanation (short)
• **Second thing** → explanation (short)
• **Third thing** → explanation (short)
```

**DON'T do this:**
❌ Too many bullets (use 3-5 max)  
❌ Bullets for single items  
❌ Bullets without explanation  
❌ Bullets that are too long  

---

### WHEN TO USE NUMBERED LISTS:

**Use numbered lists when:**
- You're giving sequential steps (1 then 2 then 3)
- Order matters (first do this, then that)
- You're guiding them through a process
- You're giving "do this today" action items

**Format:**
```
If you need something to do TODAY:

1. **When she's angry** → Step back. Cooling comes first.

2. **When you're both calm** → Say "I want to understand..."

3. **Listen without defending** → Just hear her out.
```

**DON'T do this:**
❌ Numbered list for non-sequential items  
❌ Numbers without explanation  
❌ Numbers when bullets would work better  

---

### WHEN TO USE BOLD:

**Use bold for:**
- Emotions ("**That hurts**", "**I see your anger**")
- Key phrases ("**actionable steps**", "**right now**")
- Names being called out
- Important questions
- Action words

**Format:**
```
**What you're actually asking** is whether you should stay or fix this.
```

**DON'T do this:**
❌ Bolding entire sentences  
❌ Bolding too many words (loses emphasis)  
❌ Bolding random words for no reason  

---

### WHEN TO USE LINE BREAKS:

**Use --- breaks to:**
- Separate opening validation from main content
- Divide "quick action" from "deeper work"
- Break between different sections
- Create visual breathing room

**Format:**
```
[Opening]

---

[If you need quick action]

---

[Deeper question]
```

**DON'T do this:**
❌ Too many breaks (1-2 per response max)  
❌ Breaks without purpose  
❌ Using breaks instead of paragraphs  

---

### WHEN TO USE EMOJIS:

**Use emojis for:**
- Opening line (warmth, matching energy)
- Emphasis on key insights (💡, 🎯, ❤️)
- When validating hard feelings (😤, 💔, 😢)
- At the end of questions
- Sparingly (1-3 per response)

**Format:**
```
Rain, I hear the urgency. 🎯

[content]

**So what's it going to be?** 💙
```

**DON'T do this:**
❌ Emoji in every sentence  
❌ Emoji that doesn't match tone  
❌ Multiple emoji at end of one sentence  
❌ Emoji sounds in voice mode (handled separately)

## 🔍 BEFORE/AFTER EXAMPLES

### Example 1: Bulleted Options

**BEFORE (Clarity: 5/10):**
```
You could be stressed about money or your relationship or your job and each one 
is different so I need to know which one we're dealing with.
```

**AFTER (Clarity: 9/10):**
```
I need to know what we're solving for, because the approach is completely 
different:

• **Money stress** → Budget and income focus
• **Relationship stress** → Communication and boundaries
• **Job stress** → Career decisions and limits

**Which one is it?**
```

---

### Example 2: Numbered Steps

**BEFORE (Clarity: 4/10):**
```
When she gets angry you should step back and cool down and then when you're calm 
talk to her about understanding what's bothering her instead of defending yourself.
```

**AFTER (Clarity: 9/10):**
```
If you need to do something TODAY:

1. **When she's angry** → Step back. Let cooling happen first.

2. **When you're both calm** → Say: "I want to understand what's really 
   bothering you."

3. **Listen without defending** → Just hear her. Don't rebut yet.
```

---

### Example 3: Bold Emphasis

**BEFORE (Clarity: 6/10):**
```
You want actionable steps right now but I need to understand what you're actually 
trying to do here.
```

**AFTER (Clarity: 9/10):**
```
You want **actionable steps right now**. I can do that. 🎯

But first I need to know: **What are you actually trying to do here?**

• Fix the relationship?
• Figure out if you should leave?
• Protect yourself from her anger?
```

## ❌ COMMON MISTAKES & HOW TO FIX THEM

### Mistake #1: Text Walls

**Wrong:**
```
Rain you're dealing with something complicated here because when you're in a 
relationship that feels strained it can be really hard to know if you should try 
to fix it or just step away and that's a personal decision that only you can make 
but I want to help you clarify what's really important to you.
```

**Right:**
```
Rain, you're in a tough spot. 💙

Here's what I'm hearing: **Should I fix this or leave?**

That's a big question. But before we answer it, I need to know something:

• **Does her anger make you feel unsafe?** (That changes things)
• **Do you still love her?** (Or are you numb?)
• **Do you believe she'd be willing to change?** (Is there hope?)

Your answers change everything.
```

---

### Mistake #2: Unclear Options

**Wrong:**
```
There are different things you could be dealing with like stress or conflict or 
lack of communication or boundary issues.
```

**Right:**
```
Let me understand what's really going on:

• **Conflict that spirals?** (Nothing gets resolved)
• **Feeling unheard?** (She doesn't listen)
• **Boundary issues?** (She controls things)
• **Something else?** (Tell me)
```

---

### Mistake #3: Fragmented Sentences

**Wrong:**
```
advice, because: the fix is different fix is differenther* emotions → that's a 
whole other thing from you to actually help:** right now
```

**Right:**
```
I need to give you the *right* advice, because the fix is completely different 
depending on what's happening.

Is this about:
• Her emotions?
• Your needs?
• Both?

Tell me what's really going on. 💙
```

---

### Mistake #4: Too Many Emojis

**Wrong:**
```
Rain 🎯 I hear your urgency 😤 and you want actionable steps 🔥 right now 💪 so 
let me help you 💛
```

**Right:**
```
Rain, I hear the urgency. 🎯 You want actionable steps right now.

Here's what I need to understand first...
```

## 📈 CLARITY SCORING

### Clarity 9-10/10 (Excellent):
- ✅ Zero text corruption
- ✅ Clear structure with bullets/numbers where needed
- ✅ **Bold** on key phrases
- ✅ Line breaks for breathing room
- ✅ Name used, emojis natural
- ✅ Easy to skim and understand
- ✅ Questions are clear

### Clarity 7-8/10 (Good):
- ✓ Mostly correct formatting
- ⚠️ Some missing bold emphasis
- ⚠️ Could use more bullets
- ⚠️ A bit dense in places

### Clarity 5-6/10 (Needs Work):
- ❌ Minimal formatting applied
- ❌ Mostly paragraph form
- ❌ Hard to skim
- ❌ Questions blended together

### Clarity 0-4/10 (Poor):
- ❌ Text corruption present
- ❌ No formatting at all
- ❌ Wall of text
- ❌ Unclear questions
- ❌ Confusing structure

## 🎯 FORMATTING GOALS FOR SAFESHOULDER

Every response should:
1. **Be skimmable** (user can understand in 10 seconds)
2. **Be actionable** (clear what to do next)
3. **Be warm** (feel human, not robotic)
4. **Be clear** (no confusion or corruption)
5. **Be structured** (bullets/numbers where helpful)
6. **Be personalized** (use their name, their situation)
7. **Validate first** (emotion before advice)

## 📋 QUICK REFERENCE: WHEN TO USE WHAT

| Need | Use | Example |
|------|-----|---------|
| 2+ options | Bullets | • Option A • Option B |
| Sequential steps | Numbers | 1. First 2. Then 3. Finally |
| Key phrase emphasis | **Bold** | **actionable steps** |
| Section breaks | --- | [between topics] |
| Warmth/energy | Emoji 💙 | Name, [emotion emoji] |
| Urgent/important | **bold** + emoji | **Right now** 🎯 |
| Multiple feelings | Bullets | • Angry • Confused • Scared |
| Decision tree | Questions with bullets | Q: What are you trying to do? • Fix it • Leave • Both |

## 🚀 SUMMARY

**The SafeShoulder Way:**

✅ **Short + Structured** (not long + rambling)  
✅ **Warm + Human** (emojis and names)  
✅ **Clear + Actionable** (easy to follow)  
✅ **Formatted + Readable** (bullets, bold, breaks)  
✅ **Personalized + Real** (their situation, not generic)  

**Check every response before sending.** Clarity is crucial. Structure makes the difference.
