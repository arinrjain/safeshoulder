# SafeShoulder Content Quality Standards

## Overview
SafeShoulder is not a licensed therapy service, BUT all therapeutic guidance must meet **professional-grade quality standards**. This applies to all knowledge base documents and AI responses.

---

## Content Quality Checklist

### 1. Evidence-Based Frameworks
✅ **Must include:**
- References to established therapeutic approaches (CBT, DBT, NVC, etc.)
- Explanation of WHY this approach works
- How to apply it in real life
- Research-backed concepts

❌ **Must NOT:**
- Make unsupported claims
- Present anecdotes as evidence
- Use pseudo-psychology
- Oversimplify complex issues

**Example:**
```
✅ "CBT suggests examining the thought → feeling → behavior cycle. 
   When your boss criticizes you (event), you might think 'I'm incompetent' 
   (thought), feel anxious (feeling), then avoid speaking in meetings (behavior). 
   By questioning that thought, you can change the cycle."

❌ "Just think positive and you'll feel better."
```

---

### 2. Nuance & Complexity

✅ **Must acknowledge:**
- Multiple valid perspectives
- When situations are ambiguous
- When simple solutions don't exist
- Individual differences in what helps
- When professional help is needed

❌ **Must NOT:**
- Oversimplify complex situations
- Present one-size-fits-all solutions
- Ignore legitimate challenges
- Minimize real struggles

**Example:**
```
✅ "Setting boundaries with family is necessary AND can be painful. 
   They might react badly. That doesn't mean the boundary was wrong—
   just that healthy change is uncomfortable for everyone."

❌ "Just set boundaries and everything will be fine."
```

---

### 3. Validation + Reality

✅ **Must balance:**
- Genuine validation of feelings
- Acknowledgment of actual difficulty
- Realistic assessment of change timeline
- Hope without false promises

❌ **Must NOT:**
- Dismiss real problems
- Promise quick fixes
- Minimize legitimate suffering
- Be overly optimistic

**Example:**
```
✅ "Recovering from heartbreak genuinely takes months. Your pain 
   is real. But research shows most people feel meaningfully better 
   within 6 months if they actively work on it."

❌ "You'll feel better soon!"
```

---

### 4. Professional Writing Standards

✅ **Must be:**
- Clear and well-organized
- Free of jargon (or explained if necessary)
- Consistent in terminology
- Grammatically correct
- Properly formatted

❌ **Must NOT:**
- Use filler or padding
- Have unclear structure
- Mix technical + casual language inconsistently
- Have typos or grammar errors

---

### 5. Ethical Guardrails

✅ **Must include:**
- Clear disclaimer: "This is not therapy"
- When to seek professional help
- Recognition of limitations
- Respect for user autonomy
- Confidentiality acknowledgment

❌ **Must NOT:**
- Diagnose conditions
- Prescribe medication or medical treatment
- Override user judgment
- Create dependency on AI
- Guarantee outcomes

**Example at start of relevant docs:**
```
⚠️ This guidance is not a substitute for professional therapy. 
If you're experiencing severe depression, suicidal thoughts, or 
abuse, please contact a mental health professional or crisis hotline immediately.
```

---

### 6. Practical Applicability

✅ **Must:**
- Give specific, actionable steps
- Explain WHY each step helps
- Acknowledge barriers to implementation
- Offer alternatives if primary approach doesn't fit
- Be realistic about effort required

❌ **Must NOT:**
- Be vague or abstract
- Ignore practical obstacles
- Require unrealistic effort
- Skip the "why" behind recommendations

**Example:**
```
✅ "Journaling helps by externally processing thoughts. 
   Spend 10 minutes writing whatever comes to mind—no judgment. 
   Many find 3x/week sustainable. If writing feels hard, 
   voice-record instead."

❌ "Do self-reflection regularly."
```

---

### 7. Accountability & Limitations

✅ **Every document must state:**
- What evidence supports this guidance
- What this approach CANNOT do
- When it might not apply
- When professional help is necessary
- Who this is/isn't for

❌ **Must NOT:**
- Claim universal applicability
- Hide limitations
- Promise guarantees
- Avoid discussing failures

---

## Quality Audit Checklist

Before publishing ANY knowledge base document:

- [ ] Does it reference specific therapeutic frameworks?
- [ ] Is the writing professional-grade and clear?
- [ ] Does it acknowledge complexity and nuance?
- [ ] Does it balance validation with realistic expectations?
- [ ] Are all recommendations specific and actionable?
- [ ] Does it have ethical guardrails/disclaimers?
- [ ] Is it organized logically?
- [ ] Are there typos or grammar errors?
- [ ] Does it respect user autonomy?
- [ ] Would a professional therapist recognize the approach?

---

## AI Response Quality Standards

Every response from SafeShoulder AI must meet:

✅ **Therapeutic listening:**
- Validate their SPECIFIC situation
- Show genuine understanding
- Ask clarifying questions
- Don't jump to solutions

✅ **Evidence-based guidance:**
- Reference therapeutic frameworks
- Explain WHY this approach helps
- Draw from knowledge base intelligently
- Acknowledge what's uncertain

✅ **Professional tone:**
- Warm but not casual
- Clear and organized
- No jargon without explanation
- Respectful of their experience

✅ **Ethical responsibility:**
- Know when to refer to professionals
- Don't diagnose or prescribe
- Acknowledge limitations
- Respect their autonomy

---

## Examples of Professional-Grade Content

### ✅ GOOD: Household Stress Document
- Validates real exhaustion from disrupted plans
- Explains psychological impact (control, predictability)
- References relevant concepts (perfectionism, delegation, boundaries)
- Gives specific coping strategies with rationale
- Acknowledges when professional help may be needed
- Professional writing and clear structure

### ❌ POOR: Generic Family Document
- "Family stress is hard"
- "Try to communicate better"
- No frameworks or evidence
- No acknowledgment of real barriers
- Oversimplifies complex situations
- Feels like generic self-help

---

## Continuous Improvement

Every quarter:
- [ ] Review knowledge base for quality gaps
- [ ] Get feedback from real users (Nidhi, others)
- [ ] Compare against professional resources
- [ ] Update outdated information
- [ ] Add missing nuance or evidence
- [ ] Remove or improve weak documents

---

## Final Standard

**Ask yourself:** "Would a licensed therapist approve of this guidance, even if they'd add more nuance?"

If the answer is no → rewrite it.

If the answer is yes but with caveats → add those caveats.

SafeShoulder is AI support, not therapy. But AI support done professionally. 💙
